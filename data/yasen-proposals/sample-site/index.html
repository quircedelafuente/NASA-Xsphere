Fast-moving star

Barnard's Star

The star with the fastest motion across our sky. Each frame is the same patch photographed by SPHEREx on a different visit. Watch the bright star under the amber cross: it shifts upward a little each visit, while the stars around it stay put.

Its infrared colours

Each dot is one SPHEREx photo. The telescope sees each photo at a slightly different wavelength, so together they draw the star's spectrum. Brightness here is a quick sum of 3×3 pixels, not calibrated.
