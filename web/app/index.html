    See the infrared sky change

    NASA's SPHEREx telescope photographs the whole sky every six months in 102 infrared colours. Pick a spot and this tool fetches its photos from NASA, lines them up, and plays them like a flip-book, so anything that moves or changes stands out.

    1. Search or click a test target.
    2. Watch the blink. Toggle Changes only.
    3. Or draw a zone and get a list of everything that changed, each checked against known stars and asteroids.
