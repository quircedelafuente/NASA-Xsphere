# RW Aurigae

1. A **young star (T Tauri type)** at **~140 pc** (van Leeuwen 2007), with a disk and companions, a famously irregular variable. [L✓] https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.09190 (Taurus membership not verified)
2. Has had long **dimming events**: 2010–11 (~2 mag, ~180 days), a shallow one 2012–13 (~0.7 mag, ~40 d), and 2014–2016 (>2 mag). Attributed to dust/disk material from a tidally disrupted disk. [L✓] Rodriguez et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03745; Facchini et al. 2016. A 2024 event was NOT found: do not state it. Do not link these to our SPHEREx data.
3. Static position (pm < 0.03″/yr): any change is brightness, not motion. [M]
4. Caveat: saturated (OVERFLOW 100/101 frames). Our D5 flux fell ~2.2 Jy → ~1.2 Jy (Sep 2025 → Sep 2026) but this is NOT reliable. [M, unreliable] Do not put in the video without QA.
