# WISE 0855−0714

1. **Coldest known brown dwarf, ~250 K** (paper: 225–260 K; ≈ −25 °C). Discovered by Luhman 2014 from WISE motion. [L✓] https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.6501
2. At **~2.2–2.3 pc** (~7 light-years). Parallax values: 454±45 mas (discovery), 433±15 mas (Luhman & Esplin 2014), 449±8 mas → 2.23 pc (Luhman & Esplin 2016, https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.06655), 439±2.4 mas → 2.28 pc (secondary source, original not identified). Say 'about 7 light-years'. [L✓ for 2.2–2.3 pc; rank among nearest systems not verified] Note: arXiv 1605.08620 is a photometry paper, not the astrometry source.
3. It was found precisely because it moves: **~8.1″/yr**, 1.33 px/yr. We saw −7.4″ in RA over 0.985 yr. [M]
4. It is **invisible below ~3.8 µm** and shows up only in D5/D6 (S/N 3–5 per exposure, 10–14 per epoch co-add): use `D56` and co-adds. [M]
5. Caveat: not in Gaia. Catalogue position (2010) is ~127″ (~20 px) off today → always propagate. [M]
